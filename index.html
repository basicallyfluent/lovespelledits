Before you ask

Good questions

Which edit does my manuscript actually need?

If you're unsure about the plot, the pacing or character development, you're in need of a development edit. If your manuscript reads a little clunky or feels 'off', consider a line edit. If you're just looking for a polish, you want a copyedit and a proofread.
If you're still unsure, feel free to send a general inquiry to discuss your manuscript and needs.

Do you work in Word or Google Docs?

I work in Microsoft Word, as it is the industry standard. Google Docs is available on request.

How far out are you booking?

I am currently accepting bookings from December 2026 through the end of February 2027.

What is your editing style?

I believe in kind and thoughtful discourse. My job is to clarify your vision and clean up the loose ends. Your novel is yours. My edits are only suggestions.

Do you edit spicy novels?

Spice is not a problem. I do draw the line at non-consensual encounters, abuse and anything that veers into body horror.
